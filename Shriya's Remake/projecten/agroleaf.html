01 / The Challenge

Opaque Sourcing & Produce Spoilage

Conventional supermarket distribution conceals food origins from urban consumers while forcing regional growers to bear heavy spoilage risks from uncommitted harvests.

Anonymous Origin Grocery store packaging obscures farmer identity, harvest dates, and soil stewardship.
Harvest Spoilage Growers pick crops without demand visibility, causing tragic field-level food waste.
Transit Freshness Lag Days spent in multi-tier wholesale transit leave greens limp and depleted of peak nutrients.
Rigid Subscriptions Traditional farm shares lock users into fixed boxes without dietary customization or pause controls.
Farmer Field Research · Regional Producer Interview

“When we know exactly how many bushels are reserved before harvesting, zero produce rots in our crates, and families receive greens picked that exact morning.”

— Organic Berry & Heirloom Grower, Agroleaf Cooperative

05 / Prototype Iterations

Prototyping the Pre-Harvest Experience

We tested four catalog models to educate consumers on seasonal batch ordering without causing delivery confusion.

01 · Rejected

Static Grocery Aisle Grid

Treated perishables like canned goods; users expected instant 30-minute delivery, causing dissatisfaction with fresh harvest cycles.

02 · Rejected

Individual Farm Storefronts

Required users to build multiple carts and pay separate shipping fees across 4 different farms.

03 · Rejected

Surprise Mystery Box Only

High churn from dietary restrictions; consumers wanted control over ingredients in their weekly boxes.

04 · Selected Model

Unified Seasonal Batch Feed + Customizable CSA Box

Combines multi-farm consolidation with explicit harvest picking dates and one-click item swaps.

07 / Edge Cases & State Handling

Managing Weather Volatility & Agricultural Flux

Farming is tied to real-world weather events. The UI gracefully communicates crop delays and seasonal shortages without breaking consumer trust.

Frost & Rain Delays

Transparent push notifications explain weather conditions and offer immediate equivalent organic crop substitutes.

Sold-Out Harvest Batches

Automated waitlist triggers reservations for the following week’s crop picking session in one click.

Cold-Chain Transit Alerts

Real-time temperature verification sensors display confirmation that greens stayed under 4°C during van transit.

Reusable Tote Return Loop

Driver scans canvas tote barcode on next drop-off, instantly crediting the user account deposit.

09 / Key Learnings

Sustainable Commerce Reflections

Insights on designing ethical marketplaces, supply chain transparency, and conscious consumer psychology.

  • 01
    Provenance creates premium emotional value: Connecting food to the human who planted and watered it transforms a commodity grocery order into a purposeful community relationship.
  • 02
    Flexibility sustains subscription loyalty: Empowering customers to easily swap ingredients or pause deliveries eliminates subscription anxiety and prevents cancellations.
  • 03
    Sustainability must be convenient: Eco-friendly workflows (like returnable totes) succeed only when the interaction cost for the user is virtually effortless.
