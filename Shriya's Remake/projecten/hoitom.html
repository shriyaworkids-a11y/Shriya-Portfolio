01 / The Challenge

Messy Inboxes & Payment Uncertainty

Creators and brand marketers lose valuable creative momentum managing partnerships across fragmented chat apps, vague video revision notes, and delayed net-60 wire payments.

Fragmented Briefs Deliverable requirements get scattered across untracked DMs, spreadsheets, and lost email threads.
Timestamp Ambiguity Imprecise text feedback forces creators to guess video edit timings, leading to unnecessary reshoots.
Milestone Anxiety Creators hesitate to work without guaranteed deposits; brands hesitate to release funds before review.
Disputed Metrics Static screenshots create friction over verified engagement rates and campaign conversion data.
Creator Focus Group · 350K Follower Tech Creator

“I love creating content, but chasing brands for payment two months after a video goes live is exhausting. Having funds locked in escrow before hitting record gives total peace of mind.”

— Focus group participant

05 / Prototype Iterations

Iterating on Video Asset Approvals

We evaluated multiple creative feedback models to accelerate the sign-off loop between creators and brand legal teams.

01 · Rejected

Static File Comment Feed

Text feedback lacked visual time markers, forcing creators to guess which second the brand was referencing.

02 · Rejected

Synchronous Live Review Calls

Scheduling live video calls across multiple time zones caused 4-day delays on minor thumbnail changes.

03 · Rejected

Email PDF Markup Attachments

Exporting feedback to PDFs fragmented conversations and lost version history during multi-round edits.

04 · Selected Model

In-Browser Frame Annotator + 1-Click Milestone Approval

Enables direct on-screen drawing, timestamp tagging, and instant automated payout release upon final sign-off.

07 / Edge Cases & State Handling

Mediating Creator Disputes & Platform Outages

Clear resolution frameworks prevent deadlocked deals when revisions exceed scope or posts face unexpected community guideline issues.

Scope Creep Protection

Revisions beyond agreed rounds automatically generate an itemized extra-fee modal with approved hourly rates.

Platform Guideline Flags

If an algorithm flags a sponsored post, the platform halts payout timers and prompts clean re-upload workflows.

Unresponsive Brand Review

Automated countdown timers ensure creators are paid on schedule if brands fail to review submitted deliverables.

Multi-Currency Settlement

International creators receive guaranteed payouts in their local currency without hidden conversion fees.

09 / Key Learnings

Creator Economy Product Insights

Lessons learned while designing dual-sided creator and enterprise B2B collaboration tools.

  • 01
    Financial certainty unlocks creative freedom: When creators know payment is guaranteed in escrow, they invest significantly more effort into creative storytelling and brand alignment.
  • 02
    Standardization reduces emotional friction: Codifying revision counts and usage licenses upfront prevents uncomfortable negotiations and preserves long-term brand goodwill.
  • 03
    Verified data builds unassailable trust: Replacing manually cropped analytics screenshots with direct platform API streams creates transparency that both finance and creative teams love.
