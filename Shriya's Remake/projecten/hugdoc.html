HugDoc

Pediatric Telehealth & Parent Child Care UI

A compassionate mobile healthcare concept designed in Figma, enabling anxious parents to quickly consult board-certified pediatricians, manage medical history, and book urgent virtual appointments.

Project Overview

Why I Designed HugDoc

When a child falls sick, parents experience immediate anxiety and cognitive overload. Traditional appointment portals require endless forms, slow verification, and cold bureaucratic interfaces. I designed HugDoc to provide a warm, reassuring, and immediate digital lifeline that guides parents to trusted medical care in minutes.

01 · Calming Experience

Gentle color harmony and rounded cards that reduce panic for distressed parents seeking urgent care.

02 · Rapid Doctor Matching

Filter certified pediatricians by sub-specialty, immediate availability, and clinic affiliations.

03 · Frictionless Telehealth

One-tap video call entry without app downloads or complicated link forwarding.

Key Screens

Crafted for Clarity When Every Minute Counts

Every screen utilizes Figma component variants, strict contrast compliance, and auto-layout spacing.

01 · Parent Care Dashboard

Child profile switcher, upcoming consultation cards, vaccination timelines, and single-tap urgent doctor connect.

  • Instant emergency consultation quick-action
  • Clear status badges for prescription refills and test results

02 · Pediatrician Profile & Bio

Verified medical credentials, hospital affiliations, patient reviews, and immediate appointment time slots.

  • Transparent consultation fee and accepted insurance
  • Highlighted parent satisfaction ratings and clinical badges

03 · High-Definition Video Room

Clean telemedicine video screen with floating symptom checklist, picture-in-picture preview, and in-call chat.

  • Prominent mic, camera, and switch controls
  • Live pediatrician diagnostic notes summary

04 · Guided Intake Questionnaire

Progressive multi-step form capturing fever duration, symptoms, and allergies before the virtual visit begins.

  • Single-tap chips for common childhood symptoms
  • Photo attachment for rash or swelling assessment

05 · Direct Follow-Up Messaging

Encrypted asynchronous messaging channel allowing parents to ask post-visit questions and request prescription refills.

  • Delivery and read status for high parent reassurance
  • Inline e-prescription and medication dosage cards
Learnings

Key Takeaways

What this healthcare project taught me about designing for high anxiety and high stakes.

Designing for High-Stress Scenarios

When a child is sick at 2 AM, complex navigation fails. Large touch targets and a prominent 'Book Now' anchor reduce cognitive panic.

Trust Through Transparency

Parent trust requires immediate visual proof of qualifications: medical license badges, verified reviews, and clinic affiliations.

Progressive Form Architecture

Splitting clinical intake questionnaires into sequential cards with pre-filled tags increased completion rates during user testing.
