01 / The Challenge

Botanical Confusion & Trust Hurdles

Despite proven cognitive benefits, newcomers to functional adaptogens are often alienated by clinical jargon, opaque extraction methods, and murky dosage guidelines.

Botanical Intimidation Cryptic species names and clinical terms confuse first-time wellness buyers.
Purity Skepticism Lack of accessible third-party lab verification leads shoppers to suspect filler ingredients.
Ritual Ambiguity Unclear timing instructions leave users unsure when or how to consume adaptogenic blends.
Subscription Anxiety Aggressive auto-renew traps cause high drop-off and cart abandonment at checkout.
Consumer Discovery Interview · Wellness Focus Group

“I want sharper focus and better sleep, but every mushroom supplement feels like either an intimidating pharmacy or cheap candy. I just need to know what to take and when.”

— Focus group participant

05 / Prototype Iterations

Designing the Supplement Routine Flow

We tested four checkout onboarding paradigms to balance consumer education with conversion velocity.

01 · Rejected

Categorizing products strictly by chemical extraction compounds intimidated 68% of first-time wellness shoppers.

03 · Rejected

09 / Key Learnings

Wellness E-Commerce Design Learnings

Key insights gained while designing for consumer wellness, trust building, and habit psychology.

  • 01
    Clarity drives trial in novel wellness categories: Replacing obscure botanical terms with clear daily benefits (Focus, Sleep, Energy) lowers the intimidation barrier for mainstream consumers.
  • 02
    Transparency is the best antidote to skepticism: Making third-party laboratory purity tests accessible on the product page directly dismantles consumer doubt and elevates brand credibility.
  • 03
    Design subscriptions with absolute consumer control: Allowing users to easily pause, delay, or swap products prevents cancellation regret and maximizes customer lifetime value.
